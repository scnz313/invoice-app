# Invoice App Demo Guide

## 🎯 Demo Overview

This guide walks you through the new Airbnb-inspired invoice app with business category selection and modern UI/UX features.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Welcome Screen
- **Beautiful hero section** with app logo and gradient background
- **Smooth animations** with fade-in and bounce effects
- **Feature highlights** showcasing key capabilities
- **"Get Started" button** with slide transition to category selection

### 2. Category Selection Screen
- **Grid layout** of 12 business categories
- **Search functionality** to filter categories
- **Category cards** with icons, names, and descriptions
- **Tap any category** to see detailed features modal
- **Staggered animations** for smooth loading

### 3. Business Setup Screen
- **3-step setup process** with progress indicator
- **Step 1**: Basic Information
  - Business logo upload
  - Business name and owner details
- **Step 2**: Contact Information
  - Phone, email, and address
- **Step 3**: Business Details
  - GST and PAN numbers
  - Category-specific information
- **Form validation** with real-time feedback
